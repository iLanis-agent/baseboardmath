Baseboard Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts baseboard and trim with division and rounding-up: the pieces a run takes, the runs a piece yields, the nails along a run, and the cost. It uses one named constant: 12 inches in a foot. The run lengths, the piece length, the nail spacing, the nails a point and the prices are yours to measure and read off your own plans and receipt. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your room and does not know your corners, your miters, your waste habits or your nailing practice, and it gives no verdict that an order is enough or that a job will fit. Every other number here is your own entry.

The pieces truth

Pieces: the trim run divided by the piece length, rounded up, with the feet left.

The runs truth

Runs: the pieces a count of equal runs takes, when each piece is cut into as many whole runs as fit.

The nails truth

Nails: the run in inches over the spacing, rounded up, plus 1 for the far end, times the nails a point.

The cost truth

Cost: the pieces at their price.

Baseboard Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
